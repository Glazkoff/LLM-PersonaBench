Large-MoE candidates excluded from the H17 pool, and why
========================================================
All three were staged complete and verified file-by-file, and all three were
given memory matched to their checkpoint (500-600GB) and enough GPUs. Each was
excluded only after a real readout attempt on the same protocol every other
candidate used.

zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash              306GB  3xH200  ran 7m10s  mass=nan
  Loads only via the concrete class its config names
  (Glm5NextForConditionalGeneration); AutoModelForCausalLM rejects the config.
  With that fixed it runs to completion and returns NaN logits.

Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next            336GB  4xH200  ran 14m08s mass=0.0000
  Runs to completion, places no mass on the answer tokens. Same signature as
  the Qwen3-235B-A22B-FP8 exclusion reported in App. B.

deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 156GB  2xH200  fails at first use
  No chat template. Neither DeepSeek-V4-Flash nor DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash ships
  chat_template.jinja either, so this is the repo family, not the revision.

Not excluded for convenience: six earlier probe failures on these same models
were infrastructure or caller error -- 61GB of RAM against a 336GB checkpoint,
an Auto-class mismatch, a gate written but not applied -- and were fixed and
retried before any verdict was recorded. Five of six first-round failures were
ours, one was the model.
